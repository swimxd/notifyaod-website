Your phone’s always-on display, for notifications that need your attention.

NotifyAOD uses your native always-on display for new notifications. Unlocking your phone acknowledges them; clearing the last pending notification turns AOD off.

Notification processing stays on your phone. Free covers one app of your choice; Pro, a one-time purchase, covers every app.
